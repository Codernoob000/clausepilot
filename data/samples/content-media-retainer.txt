CONTENT & MEDIA PRODUCTION RETAINER AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into on February 10, 2025, between Global Reach Media LLC ("Agency") and Freelance Content Creator ("Creator").

SECTION 1. EDITORIAL & VIDEO SERVICES
Creator shall produce four (4) long-form video packages and accompanying editorial copy per month for Agency's corporate client accounts. Agency will assign monthly editorial topics on the 1st of each calendar month.

SECTION 2. RETAINER FEES & CONTINGENT PAYMENT
Agency agrees to pay Creator a monthly retainer of $3,500.00. Payment is contingent upon Agency receiving payment from its end-client sponsor ("pay-when-paid"). If the sponsor fails to pay Agency within ninety (90) days, Agency shall have no obligation to disburse payment to Creator for the affected month.

SECTION 3. EDITORIAL REVISIONS & ACCEPTANCE
Creator shall provide unlimited revisions on all deliverables until Agency's client provides unconditional written sign-off. If a deliverable is rejected by the client, Creator shall re-record or rewrite the content within 48 hours without additional compensation.

SECTION 4. OWNERSHIP & PORTFOLIO BAN
All videos, raw b-roll footage, scripts, audio tracks, and graphic elements shall be the exclusive property of Agency. Creator shall not mention Creator's involvement in the production of any content, shall not upload any excerpts or unreleased b-roll to any portfolio, YouTube channel, or social media platform, and shall observe strict perpetual anonymity regarding all deliverables.

SECTION 5. CANCELLATION & KILL CLAUSE
Agency may cancel this retainer agreement at any time with twenty-four (24) hours email notice. Upon cancellation, Creator forfeits any claim to unbilled days or partial month retainer allocations, and all unfinished project files must be transferred to Agency immediately.

SECTION 6. INDEMNITY
Creator warrants that all content delivered is original and free from defamation, copyright claims, or privacy violations. Creator shall defend, indemnify, and hold Agency harmless against all claims, settlement costs, and legal fees arising from the publication of Creator's content.

SECTION 7. JURISDICTION
This Agreement is governed by the laws of New York County, New York.
